Wake-up call: US economy sees largest sales decline in nearly a decade.
Economists point to rising inflation and global trade tensions as key factors.
Sales have dropped a whopping 3.1% year-over-year for the first time since 2009.
Decline is affecting major sectors including electronics, clothing, and home goods.
The impact is being felt in every region of the country.
The downturn in sales marks a major shift in consumer spending behaviors.
