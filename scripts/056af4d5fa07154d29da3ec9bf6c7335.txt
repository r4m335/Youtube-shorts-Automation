What if your phone survived a fall from space—without a scratch?
RHINOSHIELD teamed up with UK’s Sent Into Space for a 100,000‑foot drop.
An iPhone 17 Pro was released from the stratosphere—out of sight, out of pain.
The RhinoShield AirX case kept it pristine—no dents, no cracks, no damage.
The stunt landed near Kiruna, Sweden, earning a Guinness World Record title.
Imagine cushioning even the toughest free‑fall drops with that protection.
