Tensions in Asia Hit Boiling Point as China Slams India Over Arunachal Pradesh Claims
China's Foreign Ministry denounces India's stance on disputed territory.
Calling it a  serious infringement on China's sovereignty and territorial integrity.
Arunachal Pradesh is a landlocked Indian state claimed by China.
India maintains control over the region but tensions remain high.
China vows to make  legitimate claims to disputed territory at any cost.
A fragile peace hangs in the balance as Asia's two superpowers clash.
