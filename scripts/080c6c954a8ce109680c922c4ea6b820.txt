Global Economy on the Brink: Iran Blocks Strait of Hormuz After US Warning
Tensions escalate as Iran warns of blocking the world's busiest oil chokepoint, strait of Hormuz.
Hormuz connects the Persian Gulf to the Arabian Sea via the Gulf of Oman.
It's a critical passageway for 20% of the world's seaborne crude oil exports, Iran's largest revenue source.
The United States has issued a warning amid growing concerns over Iran's aggressive maneuvers.
Iran's Revolutionary Guard has issued threats to block the strait in response to US sanctions.
This drastic move could trigger an oil price crisis and plunge the global economy into turmoil.
Will Iran's bold challenge to the US be a game-changer in the Middle East conflict?
