Scammers posing as migrant advocates stole £100,000 from innocent refugees in the UK last year.
Their tactics: sending direct messages on Instagram with fake job offers or financial aid.
Victims were lured with promises of employment, scholarships, or even a pathway to citizenship.
But in reality, they were asked to send money to secure their spot.
These scams are often difficult to detect as scammers use legitimate-looking profiles and language.
The Home Office warns migrants to be cautious when engaging with online strangers.
