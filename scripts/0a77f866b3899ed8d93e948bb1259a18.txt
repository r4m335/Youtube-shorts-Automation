Imagine a muscle that works itself—no pump, no sweat, just pure science magic!
Researchers from China’s Institute of Zoology grew tiny muscle grafts that exercise on their own.
In mice, those self‑gym muscles boost bone density, liver health, and even brain power.
They even produce GLP‑1, a hormone that fuels metabolism and keeps your gut happy.
Picture it as a tiny gym inside your body—no coffee needed!
And the best part? The next step is human trials, so everyone could someday benefit.
