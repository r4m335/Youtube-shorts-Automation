Unlock the hidden power in your tweets: perfect quote formatting in seconds.
To add quotes use standard double quotes  or curly quotes “ and ”.
Use single space after and before the quotes for optimal readability.
For block quotes use triple greater-than and triple less-than symbols >>> or <<<.
Footnotes on Twitter are handled with simple hashtags, e.g. #fn1.
Numbered footnotes can be denoted with superscript numbers, e.g. ¹.
Use Markdown syntax to customize font style for better readability.
