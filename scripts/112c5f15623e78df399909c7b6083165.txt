Massive Quake Rocks Indonesia, Death Toll Climbs to 47.
Strong magnitude 7.7 earthquake strikes in eastern Indonesia on August 14.
Epicenter located near the town of Malawella in Sulawesi province.
Rescue operations underway as several districts experience severe damage.
Multiple buildings and homes have been reduced to rubble.
At least 10 people trapped under debris in Malawella alone.
Your thoughts go out to the people of Indonesia during this devastating time.
