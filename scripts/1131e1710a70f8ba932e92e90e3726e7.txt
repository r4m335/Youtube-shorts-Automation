Global arms trade just got a lot more shady as a national citizen faces the music.
Chinese national Li Fang Wei pleads guilty in US court.
He smuggled millions of US-made components to China's Huawei.
The components were designed for military aircraft systems.
Wei's actions could lead to 20 years in prison and fines.
A sentencing decision is set for later this year.
But one question remains: what's the true extent of China's military influence?
