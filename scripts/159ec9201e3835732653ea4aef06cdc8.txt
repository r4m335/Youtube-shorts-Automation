MIND-BLOWING Eclipse Caught on Camera by European Space Agency from 22,000 Miles Up!
A total solar eclipse visible from Earth was spotted by the European Space Agency's crew in mid-air.
The spacecraft was orbiting 22,000 miles away from our planet when it witnessed this cosmic phenomenon.
On the spacecraft's cameras, the moon blocking the sun's light is a breathtaking sight to behold.
The eclipse was observed in a single, uninterrupted shot, thanks to the advanced technology on board.
The European Space Agency shared the incredible footage on their official website.
The stunning visual is a testament to the agency's commitment to space exploration and discovery.
