Nigeria just witnessed its largest mass wedding, shattering all expectations and defying tradition.
Over 1,000 couples exchanged vows in Kaduna state in a stunning display of unity.
The wedding was organized to promote interfaith relationships and break cultural barriers.
It is one of the first large-scale weddings to be held since the outbreak of COVID-19.
The event was attended by high-profile guests and dignitaries from across Nigeria.
Love prevailed as couples from Muslim and Christian backgrounds tied the knot.
