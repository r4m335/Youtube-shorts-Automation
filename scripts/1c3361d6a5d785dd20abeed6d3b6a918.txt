The Final Frontier of Physical Game Releases is Coming to an End
From PlayStation 3 to Xbox 360, discs ruled the gaming scene
But now, the PS3 is officially discontinued, marking a significant shift
Last week, Sony stopped producing the last PS3 game, a nostalgic goodbye
Only a few physical games left will still be released for PS3 and Xbox 360 consoles
These will be the last remaining holdouts before discs become obsolete
But one thing's left unanswered: Will we ever see a resurgence in physical game sales?
