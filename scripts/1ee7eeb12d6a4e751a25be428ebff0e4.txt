AC Milan legend Paolo Maldini joins Cagliari on a shock loan move
After spending his entire playing career at AC Milan, Maldini makes unexpected move.
36-year-old Maldini still plays for AC Milan but will now join Cagliari for six months.
The experienced defender will provide Cagliari a boost in their fight against relegation.
Maldini said the move is in line with his personal ambitions to win more titles.
Will Cagliari's addition of Maldini be enough to turn their season around?
