Meet the infamous Australian bird - your 5am alarm just flew in.
Native to eastern Australia, the kookaburra resides in dry eucalyptus forests.
These large kingfishers boast bold, white stripes on their heads, black plumage.
They've gained a peculiar reputation among bushmen, known as laughing jackasses.
The harsh, cackling call has become synonymous with the Australian outback's dawn chorus.
