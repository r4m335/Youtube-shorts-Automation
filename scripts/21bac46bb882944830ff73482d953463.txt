Washington DC on Edge as Local Council Calls for Immediate National Guard Withdrawal.
Recent calls for the withdrawal follow a weekend of violent protests in the nation's capital.
Washington DC Mayor Muriel Bowser and police chief had previously requested National Guard support.
Local lawmakers now argue the presence of troops is exacerbating the situation.
Critics point to reports of increased tensions between residents and Guardsmen on patrol.
DC City Councilmembers emphasize the need for a peaceful resolution to ongoing protests.
As the debate heightens, one question looms large: what's next for Washington DC?
