Rhine River Water Level Hits Record Low: A Crisis Unfolds in Europe's Waterway
Historic drought is taking its toll on Europe's water system.
Water levels on Rhine River have plummeted due to severe drought.
Rhine River is one of Europe's most busiest waterways responsible for trade.
The low water levels are causing transportation disruptions and economic losses.
Ship operators are forced to navigate through deeper channels to avoid grounding.
Germany and the Netherlands are the worst affected regions so far.
