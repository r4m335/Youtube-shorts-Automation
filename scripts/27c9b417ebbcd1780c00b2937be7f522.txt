Heads up, riders! New headlight laws are coming that could change how you see the road.
From April 2027, two‑wheelers must have automatic low‑beam headlamps only.
High‑intensity LEDs on bikes will drop from 150,000 to 100,000 candela.
Four‑wheelers also face lower limits, trimming bright glare for safer night drives.
Why the change? To cut dangerous glare that blinds pedestrians and other drivers.
If you ride or drive, make sure your lights meet the new rules.
