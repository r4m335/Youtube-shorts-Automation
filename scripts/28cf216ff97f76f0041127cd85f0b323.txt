Mystery and engineering collided as Antoni Gaudí unveiled his Sagrada Família vision in 1884.
In 1906, Gaudí took over the project after Eusebi Güell's initial design.
This marked a turning point, with Gaudí's innovative style shaping the construction.
He introduced a new approach using natural light and parabolic arches.
The construction accelerated, with a team of 600 workers under Gaudí's guidance.
By 1906, the central nave had risen to over 60 meters high.
But little did people know, Gaudí had planned for the Sagrada Família to take 150 years to complete.
