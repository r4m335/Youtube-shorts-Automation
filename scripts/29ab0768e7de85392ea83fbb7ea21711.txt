Millions of sensitive Google Docs documents leak online sparking widespread panic and outrage worldwide.
The leak, reportedly discovered in November 2022, exposed data from Google Workspace services for businesses.
Unsecured files contained personal identifiable information, financial records, and confidential communications.
Experts say the leak occurred as a result of an unsecured Google Cloud Storage bucket.
The security failure could have happened due to human error or inadequate technical measures.
