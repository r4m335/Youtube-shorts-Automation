Over 20 million kids worldwide suffer from peanut allergy - and you could be next.
Researchers found introducing peanuts to babies as young as 4 months old reduces risk.
The study suggests a small amount of peanut every few days lowers allergy chances by 80%.
But it's not about forcing them to eat a whole peanut, just introducing the protein.
In some cases, the allergy risk even disappears completely by age 5.
