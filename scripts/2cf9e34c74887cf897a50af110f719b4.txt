Imagine tiny homes packed in a village within LA, transforming lives of 200 residents overnight.
In partnership with local organizations, LA created this innovative tiny home village.
Each home has an average size of 250 sqft, providing basic necessity to its occupants.
This unique project addresses the issue of affordable housing in the city.
Residents have access to on-site amenities and social services for their betterment.
The tiny home village is a first step towards a larger citywide redevelopment plan.
