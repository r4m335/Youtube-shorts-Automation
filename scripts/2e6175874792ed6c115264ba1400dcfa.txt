Pakistan just created a new lifeline for the endangered Indus River dolphin!
The Punjab government just set up the Punjnad Indus River Dolphin Sanctuary.
It’s the first protected area in Punjab for this rare freshwater species.
Punjnad means “five rivers,” and the sanctuary sits where all five meet.
The stretch has the highest dolphin concentration in the province.
Conservationists and the World Wide Fund for Nature welcomed this move.
This is a huge win for biodiversity in Pakistan’s heartland.
