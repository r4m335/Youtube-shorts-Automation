Will PSG's Dominance be Stopped in Ligue 1?
Paris Saint-Germain sits top with 64 points from 24 matches played so far.
Marseille is a distant second at 47 points, a whopping 17 adrift.
Their recent loss to Monaco has raised eyebrows in the French football scene.
Reigning champions Lyon are in a mediocre fifth spot despite a decent defensive record.
But Lille and Nice are lurking, both within striking distance of the top three teams.
Will the title race become a three-horse contest or will PSG continue to reign supreme?
