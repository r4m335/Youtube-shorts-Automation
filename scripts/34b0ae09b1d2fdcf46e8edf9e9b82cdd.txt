Breaking News: Jellyfish Short Circuit French Nuclear Reactors Causing Mass Blackout
In a bizarre incident, massive jellyfish clog cooling pipes of nuclear reactors in France.
The reactors, at the Tricastin Nuclear Power Plant, automatically shut down to prevent disaster.
No injuries or radioactive leaks were reported; the reactors were safely offline within hours.
Experts are attributing this unusual event to rising ocean temperatures and climate change.
The plant owners, EDF, are investigating the cause and implementing countermeasures.
Jellyfish have been known to disrupt operations at these facilities before, but never to this extent.
