Dramatic rescues and stunning mountains: Swiss volunteer firefighters face it all.
Volunteer firefighters in Switzerland are on the front lines, responding to emergencies 24/7.
From dense forests to picturesque villages, they risk everything to save lives.
Their bravery is impressive, but behind the scenes, they face a tough reality: equipment shortages and inadequate training facilities.
The situation is dire, with some stations struggling to cover long response times due to understaffing.
Volunteer firefighters work tirelessly to bridge the gap, but it's an uphill battle.
Will innovative solutions and community support be enough to help these heroes in need?
