Spanish PM Sanchez Removal Odds Now Shorten to 1 in 10.
Sanchez remains in office despite growing discontent from party members.
A year ago, his removal odds stood at 1 in 4.
The unexpected drop in odds has left many stunned.
Analysts claim it's due to improved economy numbers and reduced polarization.
Investors are cautiously optimistic about Spain's future under Sanchez's leadership.
