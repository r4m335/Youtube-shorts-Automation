Shocking twist in Chad's politics: President Mahamat Déby pardons his main rival, Idriss Déby's son, N'gadal.
Opposition leader, Ngarlejy Yorongar, is also set free after years of detention.
The move is seen as a bid to ease tensions and promote national unity.
Déby's pardon is a surprising shift in his stance against Déby's loyalists.
No details were given on the terms or conditions of the pardon.
The opposition has long demanded the release of Ngarlejy Yorongar.
