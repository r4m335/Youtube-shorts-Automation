Imagine a future where unmanned boats defend our coastlines and save precious lives.
The Pentagon announces a groundbreaking challenge to develop these cutting-edge vessels.
The US Department of Defense invites innovative minds to participate in their unmanned boat competition.
The goal is to create boats that can stay at sea for weeks, carry heavy cargo, and operate independently.
These unmanned boats must detect and neutralize underwater threats without a human crew.
Teams will compete for millions in funding to turn their ideas into reality.
Who will revolutionize maritime security? Will your favorite team take the prize?
