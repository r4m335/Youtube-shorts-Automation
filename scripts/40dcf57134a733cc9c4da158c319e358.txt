Breaking News: 4,100 people infected with Ebola in the Democratic Republic of Congo
Cases are rising in North Kivu, a densely populated region where outbreak began two years ago
Health organizations on the ground are working tirelessly to contain the spread
Over 2,200 people have lost their lives to the deadly virus so far
Local communities are taking matters into their own hands with increased awareness campaigns
Experts warn that climate change and urbanization are fueling the outbreak's persistence
