Military grade protection has arrived for Mexico's prized avocado crop production
Mexico's avocado industry is valued at over 20 billion US dollars annually export
Guerrilla groups have targeted lucrative avocado facilities and transportation routes often
The Mexican government has vowed to increase security measures to protect farmers safety
Troops deployed to major avocado producing regions have been armed with riot shields
Their mission is to safeguard the country's avocado crop from vandalism and theft risks
