Amazon's Texas Data Center to Emit More CO₂
Breaking: Massive emissions increase expected from Amazon's Texas data center expansion project
Located in Taylor, Texas, it's now the company's 8th data center in the US state
The data center generates around 50 million pounds of CO₂ annually already
Projected emissions for the new expansion could reach a staggering 100 million pounds per year
That's equivalent to 1 million extra cars on the road every year
Environmental activists and local residents raise concerns over the project's impact
What's the trade-off between rapid growth and climate protection?
