Julián Álvarez's future hangs in balance as Atletico Madrid prepares an offer.
The 23-year-old Argentine sensation has been impressing on loan at Bayern Leverkusen.
Alvarez is set to return to Manchester City this summer but Atletico is desperate.
Club officials are reportedly ready to launch a €100 million bid for the player's services.
The deal would make him one of the most expensive signings in Atlético history ever.
Will Juve's bid be enough to pry Alvarez away from City and Atletico talks start?
