Tobacco farmers in North Macedonia just revealed a game-changing secret: night harvesting.
A labor shortage forced them to adapt, switching to nighttime operations.
Night harvesting reduces damage to leaves and increases yields.
Farmers report a 15% boost in productivity using specialized equipment.
With expert training, yields can be boosted up to 25%.
North Macedonia becomes a leader in innovative tobacco farming practices.
