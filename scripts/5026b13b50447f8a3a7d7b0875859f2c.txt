Police stunned by man arrested for wearing full superhero costume on rooftop.
John Doe, 32, was a concerned neighbor who took roof vigilance to the next level.
He donned full Batman regalia, night vision goggles, and binoculars to survey his block.
His neighbors found his antics humorous at first, but they soon felt harassed.
The authorities arrived after a noise complaint about suspicious roof activity at 2 am.
