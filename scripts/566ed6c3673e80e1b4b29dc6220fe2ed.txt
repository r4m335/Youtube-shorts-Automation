Warning signs of instability as Pakistan ranks 152nd on global peace index, slipping further down.
A peaceful nation is everyone's dream, yet we find ourselves in a precarious position.
The Global Peace Index assesses 163 countries based on various parameters like conflict, crime, and terrorism.
Our ranking takes into account issues like sectarian violence, kidnapping, and terrorist attacks in our country.
The numbers are alarming, but what's more concerning is the lack of effort to address these issues.
We need a collective effort to create a sense of community and foster trust among our citizens.
