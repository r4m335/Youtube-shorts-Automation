Imagine your computer's brain working in lightning-fast harmony, thanks to tiny instructions hidden everywhere.
I/O operations are the backbone of data transfer, reading, and writing.
The key to understanding them is through diagramming - it's like reading a blueprint.
Input (I) devices send data to the CPU, while Output (O) devices receive it.
Diagram 1: Sequential I/O - data travels through the CPU like a tiny pipeline.
Diagram 2: Parallel I/O - multiple data streams flow in and out at the same time.
But, did you know, some computers use hybrid I/O models to balance performance and power efficiency?
