Wake up, Americans: your paycheck just got a lot stronger overnight in the UK.
U.S. workers are now earning the same as their British counterparts in major tech jobs.
Apple employees in the States make the same as those in London.
Google workers, Tesla staff, and Microsoft employees are all on an equal pay scale.
This shift in wages is largely due to the strong U.S. dollar.
As a result, British workers are being pulled across the pond.
