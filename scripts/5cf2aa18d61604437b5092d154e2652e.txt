Liverpool lands HUGE transfer coup in a major signing shakeup
Ronald Araujo set to join Reds on a season-long loan from Barcelona.

Deal worth reported £5 million loan fee plus add-ons.
Araujo will bolster Reds' defense against strong Premier League opposition.
His pace, power, and aerial presence a welcome addition to Jurgen Klopp's squad.
