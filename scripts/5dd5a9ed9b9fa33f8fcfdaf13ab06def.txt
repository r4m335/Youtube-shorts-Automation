South Sudanese migrants lose pathway to temporary stay in the United States indefinitely.
Temporary Protected Status, granted to over 70000 individuals, terminates.
Status was first introduced in 1997 following brutal South Sudanese civil war.
Eligible migrants given 18 months to find alternative US visa options.
US Department of Homeland Security says no further action needed for current holders.
Status termination leaves thousands uncertain about future in the US.
