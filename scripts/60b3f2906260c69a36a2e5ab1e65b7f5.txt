Mosquitoes are not just biting, they're now a target of China's super laser technology.
Scientists in China have created a high-powered laser to kill mosquitoes in mid-air.
The cutting-edge system uses a 1-kilowatt laser beam to eliminate the insects.
Researchers claim a 90% success rate in killing mosquitoes with the new technology.
This could bring significant relief to people in regions plagued by mosquito-borne diseases.
China has already started testing the system in several cities across the country.
Imagine having laser technology to protect you from mosquitoes - a game-changer for public health in Asia and beyond.
