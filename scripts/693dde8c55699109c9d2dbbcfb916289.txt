Temperature records just got flushed like a garbage scroller in our global oceans.
NASA reports a record-breaking 1.04°F (0.58°C) rise in ocean temperatures since the 1980s.
That's 40% of the world's surface breaking a new high mark for heat.
These scorching temperatures are affecting marine life and food chains worldwide.
Extreme weather conditions like Hurricane Ian are intensifying due to warmer ocean waters.
The world's largest ocean, the Pacific, saw a 0.7°F (0.39°C) jump in temperature alone.
Can our climate goals stay on course as ocean temperatures continue to skyrocket?
