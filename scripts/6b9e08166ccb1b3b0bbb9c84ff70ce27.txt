BREAKING: Chaos Erupted in Los Angeles Homeless Encampment Where 1 Man Lost His Life.
A tense standoff unfolded between police and a man at a California homeless encampment yesterday evening.
The 32-year-old suspect allegedly threatened officers with a knife before the fatal confrontation.
Multiple units responded to the scene with crisis negotiators trying to de-escalate the situation.
The situation quickly escalated, and gunfire was exchanged resulting in the man's death.
The Los Angeles Police Department is investigating the shooting.
No police officers were injured during the incident.
