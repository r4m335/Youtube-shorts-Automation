Ever wonder why a live‑action Coyote film is the funniest thing since 2006?
Will Forte and Lana Condor just dropped a sneak peek of Coyote vs Acme.
They’re playing Wile E.’s legal defense, so expect courtroom chaos and cartoon gags.
Directed by Dave Green, written by Samy Burch with a story by James Gunn and Jeremy Slater.
The movie was shelved by Warner Bros. in 2023, but it’s back for a fall 2026 release.
Forte says the goal is to show that even the most clueless characters can find redemption.
Condor adds that the film will leave audiences laughing while reflecting on perseverance.
