BREAKING NEWS: Chelsea's Enzo Fernandez Transfer Saga Heats Up as Deadline Looms
Chelsea sets 30th of August as deadline for new signing Enzo Fernandez transfer
Fernandez, a Argentine midfielder, joined Chelsea for a record-breaking €121 million transfer
Deal has sparked excitement among fans, but also raised eyebrows over the hefty transfer fee
Chelsea faces stiff competition from European giants Liverpool and Manchester City
Fernandez was instrumental in Benfica's Portuguese league title win last season
Chelsea's manager, Mauricio Pochettino, will be hoping to replicate this form in England
The wait begins, will Enzo seal his deal in time, or will Chelsea's deadline leave him uncertain?
