Get ready, drama lovers—Tencent’s newest epic is rolling on set!
Li Landi and He Yu are starring in the historical romance Qian Ye Qi Tan.
It’s adapted from the popular Tencent manhua and donghua Psychic Princess.
Filming starts July 30, 2026, with 30 episodes planned.
Director Zhao Yi Long brings his Eastern Fantasy flair, while Ji Sang Rou writes the script.
Expect romance, laughs, and supernatural twists—perfect for fans of FKShi.
