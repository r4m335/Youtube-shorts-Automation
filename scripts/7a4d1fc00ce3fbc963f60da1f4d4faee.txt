Iran's grip on the Middle East just got a whole lot tighter.
Tehran now uses threats of force to maintain dominance over Strait of Hormuz.
Control of this vital oil shipping route has long been a source of conflict.
The strait allows over 20% of the world's oil to pass through its waters daily.
Stricter inspections and sanctions have further strained relations between Iran and its neighbors.
Iran's aggressive military maneuvers are making other nations think twice about intervening.
Will the world eventually push back, or will Iran remain in control?
