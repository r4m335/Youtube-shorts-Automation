Majestic cosmic pillars stand guard, holding secrets of star birth for 6,500 light-years.
In the Eagle Nebula, these towering structures are the epicenter of celestial rebirth.
Astronomers believe powerful stellar winds compress interstellar gas, creating the pillars.
As this gas collapses, new stars burst forth – a stunning cosmic cycle of life.
