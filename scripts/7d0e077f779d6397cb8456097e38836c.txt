Diplomatic Cold War Thaws as Israel and Venezuela Re-Establish Ties After 61 Years
Israel and Venezuela resume ambassador-level diplomatic relations to boost economic cooperation
The decision follows years of efforts by the Venezuelan government to strengthen ties
Ties were severed in 1962 following a dispute over the Suez Crisis
The move aims to stimulate trade and investment in key sectors like agriculture
This surprise breakthrough could have significant implications for the Middle East region
The re-established diplomatic channel will enable the two nations to collaborate on global issues
