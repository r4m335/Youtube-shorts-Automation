Millions of lives hang in the balance as Alzheimer's claims another victim every 65 seconds in the US.
The FDA has just approved Amyvid's companion agent, florbetapir F 18, for use in diagnostic imaging.
This new imaging agent will help doctors identify Alzheimer's patients earlier and with greater accuracy.
Florbetapir F 18 detects beta-amyloid plaques, a hallmark of the disease, in the brain.
Early detection is key to slowing disease progression and improving patient outcomes.
Researchers say this breakthrough could pave the way for more effective treatments and therapies.
Could this revolutionary imaging agent be the game-changer we so desperately need to defeat Alzheimer's?
