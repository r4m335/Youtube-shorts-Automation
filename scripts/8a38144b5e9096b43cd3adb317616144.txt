Magic is real and it's saving the world's power one spell at a time in UK
Fans of wizarding world are using magic to fuel UK's new sustainable energy project
Inspired by Hogwarts greenhouses, the project harnesses renewable solar power
Community gardens and schools will receive free solar panels
The project aims to power 100,000 UK homes with pure magic energy - err, we mean solar energy
But magic wasn't the only thing powering this project
Community donations from fans worldwide helped make it possible
The question on our minds now - which UK city will be the first to fully cast the 'renewable' spell?
