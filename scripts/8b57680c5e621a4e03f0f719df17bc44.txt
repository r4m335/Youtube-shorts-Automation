India's Forests are Vanishing Before Our Eyes, and the Numbers are Shocking
Indian government set ambitious goal to increase forest cover by 33 million hectares by 2030
But recent data reveals a staggering 98% shortfall in meeting this target
Only 12.2 million hectares of forest have been added so far
The rate of deforestation is alarming, with over 2 million hectares lost each year
The consequences of this environmental disaster are far-reaching, affecting millions
A healthy forest ecosystem is crucial for India's biodiversity and climate goals
Will India's leaders come up with a plan to revive the country's rapidly dwindling forests?
