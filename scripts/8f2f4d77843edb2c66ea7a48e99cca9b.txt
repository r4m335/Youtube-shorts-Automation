UPI transactions now hit you where it hurts the most, in your wallet.
The Indian government announces a new policy, allowing merchants to levies charges.
This change comes as a shock to customers, accustomed to free transactions.
Until now, merchants weren't allowed to charge fees for UPI payments.
However, with 45% of transactions valued over Rs 200, losses mounted for some merchant businesses.
Will you be paying more for your next online purchase?
