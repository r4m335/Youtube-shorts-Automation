Hungary takes drastic measure to save nuclear plant
The Danube Riverbed is being raised by 20 feet in Hungary's Paks region
This massive infrastructure project aims to prevent future flooding
The flooding caused by Danube's high water levels threatened two Paks Nuclear Power Plants
With 2 billion euros invested in the project Hungary seeks to enhance safety
Nuclear power accounts for a significant portion of Hungary's electricity production
Will this plan prevent a future nuclear disaster and secure Hungary's energy needs?
