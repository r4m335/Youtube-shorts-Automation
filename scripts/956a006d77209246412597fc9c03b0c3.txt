BREAKING: Spotify just shook the music industry with a new rule for AI-generated tracks.
Spotify Creator Labeling rule now requires human involvement in AI music production.
This change affects artists using AI music generators like Amper Music, AIVA, and Landr.
The goal is to clearly differentiate between human and AI-created content.
Spotify aims to maintain the trust of users with accurate metadata.
