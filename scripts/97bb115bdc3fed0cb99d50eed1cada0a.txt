Unbelievable Disaster Strikes Ancient Cheese Storage Sites in Switzerland
These historic Cheese Banks stored thousands of wheels for centuries underground
A combination of high temperatures and drought left them extremely vulnerable
The once-impenetrable sites began to show signs of significant water damage
Some of these treasured wheels were severely affected by rising mold and fungus
Local authorities scrambled to save what's left of the priceless dairy heritage
Efforts are underway to preserve any edible cheese remaining
