Brits Abandon Idea of Immigration as Key to UK's Economic Success
A recent government proposal suggests UK prosperity can thrive without foreign workers.
Lowest birth rates in decades and aging population pose a significant workforce challenge.
The UK's population will peak in the next decade due to these factors.
Immigration will no longer be prioritized for economic growth.
Instead, the focus will be on education and training British youth.
