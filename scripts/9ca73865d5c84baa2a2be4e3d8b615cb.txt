Shock: France Reverses Total Ban on Social Media, but with Key Restrictions
French government scraps previous plan to block all social media sites nationwide
New rules require tech companies to remove hate speech and extremist content within 24 hours
Major social media platforms like Facebook, Twitter, and Google are exempt from the block
Instead, they must comply with new EU regulations regarding hate speech and content moderation
France wants to create safer online environment while preserving free speech rights
Critics fear the new rules may stifle online expression and create 'shadow' accounts
