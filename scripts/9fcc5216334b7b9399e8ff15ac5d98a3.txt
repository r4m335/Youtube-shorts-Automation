A Singapore-flagged ship has just collided with the Hooghly River bank in India's Port of Haldia.
The incident is said to have occurred on Tuesday evening.
Reports indicate the ship was carrying a cargo of steel and coal.
The cause of the accident remains unclear at this time.
Rescue teams were quickly dispatched to the scene for assistance.
The extent of damage to both the ship and the riverbank is now being assessed.
A thorough investigation will be conducted to determine the cause of this tragic incident.
