Ever wonder how every Batman movie stacks up? Let’s count ’em down!
We start at the bottom with Adam West’s campy 1966 TV movie—classic, but kinda cheesy.
Next, the 1992 Tim Burton flick: Batman Returns, dark but stylish, with Michael Keaton.
Then we hit the 2005 reboot, Batman Begins, a gritty, grounded start to Nolan’s trilogy.
Followed by The Dark Knight (2008)—the best, with Heath Ledger’s Joker.
After that, The Dark Knight Rises (2012) gives a solid, if slightly tired, finale.
Jumping ahead, Batman v Superman (2016) feels like a side story, not a pure Batman film.
That’s the lineup—feel free to vote for your favorite!
