Imagine Unleashing a Deadly Firebomb in the Streets of 1936
The original Molotov cocktail was designed by Aitatari Aunio in Finland.
Used by Finnish rebels, it consisted of a glass bottle filled fuel.
The bottle was typically filled with a flammable liquid such as gasoline.
This improvised explosive device ignited when thrown, spewing flames everywhere.
A piece of this dark history now rests in a museum exhibit.
