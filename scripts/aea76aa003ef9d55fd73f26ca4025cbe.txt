Cryptocurrency revolution takes hold in El Salvador: 1 BTC bought every day.
The government's move is aimed at Bitcoin adoption nationwide.
Since September 2021, the country has been buying Bitcoin daily.
This strategic move aims to create a stable price floor.
The daily investment amounts to around $100,000 in Bitcoin.
Experts believe it's a bold but clever financial strategy.
The question remains: can this experiment change the crypto game?
Will other countries follow El Salvador's lead in embracing Bitcoin?
