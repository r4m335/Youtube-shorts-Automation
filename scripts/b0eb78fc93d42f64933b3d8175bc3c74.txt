Taiwan is about to unleash its deadliest defence system yet - a fleet of killer drones!
Taiwan's military has accelerated plans to develop drone swarms to counter Chinese aggression.

Taiwan's drones will operate at low altitudes, making them harder to detect and track by enemy forces.
The drone defence plan is expected to be completed within the next two years.
