Bills not breaking the bank, Mexico's economy is finally gaining traction fast.
For months, the country struggled with a stubbornly high inflation rate.
But recent figures show annual inflation has plummeted to a 6-year low mark.
This significant drop can be attributed to monetary policies implemented by the central bank.
They've successfully kept a lid on prices by regulating money supply.
