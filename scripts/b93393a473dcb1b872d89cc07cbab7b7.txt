Billion-Dollar Empire Unleashed: FCC Dismantles Broadcast Station Ownership Cap
Long-standing federal rule restricts media companies from owning too many TV stations and radio.
That regulation limits companies to owning no more than 39% of U.S. media market.
Historically, larger stations often struggled to expand operations under such strict guidelines.
The newly lifted restrictions pave the way for massive consolidation and growth in the industry.
Will this shift mark the beginning of a media giant's unstoppable rise to dominance?
