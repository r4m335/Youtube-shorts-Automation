Meet Spain's haunting anthem that's been sung in silence for 125 years.
The iconic 'La Marcha Real' is a symbol of national pride.
It's been the country's national anthem since 1908.
Despite its importance, it doesn't have any official lyrics.
Its origins are a mystery that still sparks debate among historians.
Some believe the melody is based on a traditional folk song.
Whatever the reason, 'La Marcha Real' has become synonymous with Spanish culture.
