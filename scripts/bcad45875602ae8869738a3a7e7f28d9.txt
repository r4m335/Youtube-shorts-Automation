Millions of coffee cups around the world are facing a major shortage.
A massive earthquake in Colombia has shaken the country, crippling its coffee production.
Coffee beans are crucial to Colombia's economy and exportation.
The country's coffee industry supplies over 10% of the world's coffee.
Flooded crops and damaged plantations mean many coffee farms are ruined.
Farmers are now racing against time to salvage what's left of their crops.
Can Colombia's coffee lovers find new sources before the market runs dry?
