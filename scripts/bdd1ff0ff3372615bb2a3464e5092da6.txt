After a two-year absence from acting in films due to drama and controversy.
James Jonathan Majors is returning to the big screen with major momentum.
Having starred in Netflix's Ant-Man series, Majors gained massive recognition instantly.
However, he had a high-profile arrest and an ongoing court case in 2023.
Now cleared of all charges, Majors is looking forward to starting anew.
Will he bounce back stronger than ever, or has his comeback been compromised?
