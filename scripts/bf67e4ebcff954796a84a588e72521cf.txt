BOMBSHELL: FCC just axed a rule that controlled the media giants for decades
The Federal Communications Commission quietly removed a crucial rule on media ownership limits
This regulation, known as the National Broadcasting Ownership Rule, dated back to the 1970s
It previously barred companies from owning more than 40% of a market's TV stations nationwide
Critics argue this move will let huge corporations dominate local news and viewpoints
