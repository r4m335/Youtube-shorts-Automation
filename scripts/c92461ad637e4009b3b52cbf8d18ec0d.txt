Turbulent Turkey makes U-turn on decades-long Kurdish conflict. Parliament passes historic law to dissolve PKK.
Turkey's parliament votes overwhelmingly in favor of a law dissolving the Kurdish Workers' Party.
The law aims to bring long-awaited peace and stability to the embattled region.
Amnesty will be offered to rebels who renounce violence and surrender their arms.
Turkish President Erdogan hails the move as a groundbreaking step towards peace.
