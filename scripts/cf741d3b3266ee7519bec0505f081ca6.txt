BREAKING FOOTBALL NEWS Crystal Palace Secures Surprise Deal for Anan Khalaili
Palace seals the deal on highly-rated Norwegian youngster Anan Khalaili for an undisclosed fee.
Khalaili joins the Eagles after impressing scouts in the Swedish Allsvenskan league.
The 19-year-old winger will look to make an immediate impact at Selhurst Park.
His pace and skill will add depth to the Palace attack alongside Wilfried Zaha.
Palace fans are eagerly awaiting Khalaili's debut in the Premier League.
