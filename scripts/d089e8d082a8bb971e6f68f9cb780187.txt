Beneath Brussels streets, a $20 million gold discovery changes everything
Construction workers on a Belgian project stumbled upon an incredible find
The discovery was made at the site of a former medieval town
Gold coins and bars dating back to the 17th century are among the treasures
The exact origin and ownership of the gold is yet to be determined
Belgian authorities are working with historians to verify the discovery
The gold is believed to have been hidden during a period of great turmoil
Now the world waits to uncover more secrets from this incredible trove
