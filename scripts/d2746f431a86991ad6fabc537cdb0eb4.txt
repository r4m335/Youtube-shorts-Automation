Imagine an Earth where every winter was a balmy paradise, warm waters never froze.
To achieve this, scientists propose moving our planet 20% farther from the Sun.
They've discovered a massive hollow asteroid nearby, the key to their plan unfolding.
This asteroid's massive gravitational pull would stabilize Earth's new orbit, no chaos expected.
The energy released from the asteroid's breakup would create a massive solar power source.
But would humanity be willing to take this unprecedented leap into a new cosmic reality?
