Millions of people in Spain's major cities are going dark to witness history unfolding above them.
A total solar eclipse, the rarest and most awe-inspiring celestial event, casts a shadow over Europe's southern coast.
People flock to cities like Madrid, Barcelona, and Valencia to experience the fleeting moment of totality.
Astronomers predict this eclipse will be visible to millions across western Europe and north Africa.
Many sky gazers have traveled from far and wide to experience this once-in-a-lifetime spectacle.
Witnessing a total solar eclipse is often described as a transcendent and spiritual experience by those who have seen it.
