Breaking News: USS Abraham Lincoln Returns to Norfolk After Record-Breaking 9-Month Deployment
USS Abraham Lincoln has ended its historic 9-month deployment in the Middle East.
The Nimitz-class supercarrier sailed into the homeport of Naval Station Norfolk in Virginia.
The ship's crew successfully conducted more than 26,000 flight operations and launched over 3,000 sorties.
During its deployment, the Abraham Lincoln served as the flagship of Carrier Strike Group 12.
The USS Abraham Lincoln returned home to a hero's welcome with family and friends waiting to greet the crew.
With a new deployment on the horizon, the crew is now undergoing well-deserved R&R.
