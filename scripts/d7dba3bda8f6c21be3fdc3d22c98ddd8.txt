Dominic Sessa lands the role of a lifetime portraying Anthony Bourdain.
The Tony Award-winning actor will star in a highly anticipated biopic.
This film explores the life of Anthony Bourdain, a celebrated author.
Bourdain's memoir 'Kitchen Confidential' exposed the culinary world's dark side.
His food and travel documentaries captivated audiences worldwide, breaking barriers.
Now, Dominic Sessa will bring this complex and charismatic figure to life on screen.
