The world is running out of space: 55.7 people per square kilometer.
That's the alarming new record of global population density.
The numbers are in and the future looks more crowded.
By 2050, over 70% of the world will live in urban areas.
Cities will face unprecedented strain on resources and infrastructure.
With rising competition for space and limited supply of land.
Can you imagine cities with 10 million people on just 1,000 square kilometers?
