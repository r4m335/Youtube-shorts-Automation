What Would Happen if 30 NATO Member Countries Suddenly Declared War on Russia?
NATO's collective defense pact, Article 5, holds all 30 members to respond in case of an attack.
Its invocation is not a matter of if but when, given rising tensions between Russia and the West.
Article 5 has never been invoked in its 73-year history.
But now some security experts warn of a 4% chance it could happen within the next two years.
The likelihood increases with Russian aggression and an escalated NATO build-up in Eastern Europe.
While unlikely, a hypothetical Article 5 invocation could lead to a catastrophic global conflict.
The world waits with bated breath for a development that would have far-reaching consequences.
