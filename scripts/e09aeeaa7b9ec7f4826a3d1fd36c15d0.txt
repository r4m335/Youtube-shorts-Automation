Price Shock Alert for Airtel Users, Your Favourite Plans Just Got Way More Expensive!
Airtel recently made some crucial changes to their mobile plans.
Many popular plans now come with a hike in the monthly charges.
The base Rs 179 plan now costs Rs 249, with data benefits reduced too.
Customers also face increased international calling rates and lower data speeds.
For some users, the price increase is as high as 75%.
Your favourite mobile carrier has clearly upped the ante.
So, are you prepared to pay more for reliable connectivity?
