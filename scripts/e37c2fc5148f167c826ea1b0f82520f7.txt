Auto loan demand in the US has just smashed records with a staggering 5.8% increase.
In March alone, American families bought over 14 million new cars.
A major factor behind this surge was an aggressive rate-cutting policy.
Banks are competing fiercely to offer the lowest interest rates possible.
The Federal Reserve also reduced its lending rates, encouraging growth.
As a result, the average auto loan has seen a price drop of nearly 12% so far this year.
Whether this trend will affect the long-term price stability of cars remains uncertain.
