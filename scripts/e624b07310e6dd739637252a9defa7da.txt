Johnny Depp's Pinky Finger Secret Finally Exposed: Was it Genius or a Quirk?
We're celebrating 25 years since the iconic 2005 Willy Wonka film with Tim Burton.
The eccentric director has now revealed that he intentionally kept a unique aspect of Johnny Depp's performance.
This distinctive mannerism, a constantly tucked-in pinky finger, took 100+ takes to capture per scene.
Crew members often missed it in early shots, thinking it was an accident or bad acting.
But Director Burton wanted it, saying it added to Wonka's offbeat charm.
The film's cast and crew agree – it's a testament to Depp's incredible method acting.
