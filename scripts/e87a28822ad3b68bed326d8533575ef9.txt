Imagine calling 911 in New Orleans, but an AI answers instead!
The city's Emergency Medical Communications Center made a shocking move.
Replacing human 911 operators with AI-powered chatbots.
These advanced systems can analyze caller data and dispatch aid.
A city of 393,000 residents is relying on technology like never before.
Critics argue AI may struggle with emotional distress or language barriers.
However, the new system promises to speed up response times and reduce costs.
Will AI be the future of emergency services, or a nightmare in the making?
