Bizarre artworks from ancient civilizations reveal dark secrets about demons disguising themselves.
From the intricate carvings of Mesopotamia to the mysterious frescoes of South America, demons are depicted in various guises.
These beings of the underworld appear as beautiful maidens, seductive spirits, or even noble dignitaries.
Their disguises were said to deceive both gods and mortals alike, making them nearly unrecognizable.
One such artwork, The Lamentation of Ur from ancient Sumeria, shows a demon dressed as a goddess.
These depictions offer a unique glimpse into the mythology of the past.
