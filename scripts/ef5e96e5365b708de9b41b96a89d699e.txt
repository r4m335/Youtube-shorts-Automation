Breakout young goalkeeper James Trafford gets his dream chance at Leeds United.
After impressing at Burnley, Trafford needs regular first-team action to thrive.
He joins Marcelo Bielsa's under-pressure side in a bid to solidify the position.
Trafford will face stiff competition from Illan Meslier, but he's ready for the test.
With Leeds struggling to find consistency, the loan deal is a risk worth taking.
Now can Trafford be the hero Leeds need, or will he falter under the pressure?
