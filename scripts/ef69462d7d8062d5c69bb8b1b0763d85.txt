Murder mystery surrounds innocent beauty on tragic birthday.
JonBenet Ramsey born December 6, 1990, a bright start.
Her parents, John and Patsy, beamed with pride and love.
But just six years later, horrific crime shook nation.
Theories and conspiracies swirled around her unsolved murder case.
One question remains unanswered, what happened to little JonBenet?
Was justice finally served, or is the truth still hidden?
