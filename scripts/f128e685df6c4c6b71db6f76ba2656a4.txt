U.S. Retail Sales Just Plummeted - A 3.2% Drop in June is the BIGGEST DECLINE EVER.
Retail sales in the United States saw a staggering downturn of 3.2% last month.
This marks the largest decline in over 12 years, outpacing economists' predictions.
The news is sending shockwaves through the market as investors scramble for answers.
The National Retail Federation attributes this slump to rising interest rates and inflation.
Meanwhile, consumer spending, a key driver of economic growth, continues to falter.
As retailers adjust to this new reality, one question remains: What's next for the struggling retail sector?
