Breaking News: WNBA Takes a Stand on the Age-Old Debate about Who Exactly is a Woman.
The league is redefining 'woman' to include individuals who identify as female.
This change brings the WNBA in line with other prominent sports leagues globally.
It aims to create a more inclusive environment for its players and fans alike.
The move follows recent discussions around trans and non-binary athletes' participation.
WNBA Commissioner Cathy Engelbert expressed her support for this updated definition.
The shift marks a significant step forward in promoting equality and acceptance.
Now, the questions are: what does this mean for sports, society, and identity?
