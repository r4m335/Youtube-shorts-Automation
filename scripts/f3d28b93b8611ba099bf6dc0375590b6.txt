Yo, your views just got a big makeover!
YouTube's counting system is changing—starting Aug 24, every public view registers as soon as the video starts.
Regular videos will now use the same instant view rule that Shorts already use.
This means fewer confusing differences in view tallies across formats.
Creators will see a clearer picture of their reach without waiting for the 30‑second rule.
Make sure your analytics are updated, because your numbers will look different overnight.
