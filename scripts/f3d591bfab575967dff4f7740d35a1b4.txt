Manchester Under Siege: Vape Shops Shut Down Overnight
Andy Burnham, Mayor of Manchester, takes drastic action against 20 vape shops.
The shops have been ordered to close immediately, effective immediately.
Mayor Burnham targets the shops, saying they're breaking council rules.
The move aims to protect underage smokers and curb nicotine addiction.
The city council says it's the first step in a larger crackdown.
