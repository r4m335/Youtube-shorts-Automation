Hackers hit CEVA Logistics, exposing sensitive data of hundreds of top European companies.
CEVA Logistics is a leading global supply chain and freight management company.
Their data breach is reportedly an unauthorized access to sensitive employee information.
CEVA Logistics quickly took down the compromised systems to prevent further breaches.
The company has not disclosed the amount of data exposed, sparking concerns over privacy.
CEVA Logistics will be conducting an investigation to find out how the breach happened.
If you work with a CEVA Logistics partner, you could be at risk, check your inbox.
