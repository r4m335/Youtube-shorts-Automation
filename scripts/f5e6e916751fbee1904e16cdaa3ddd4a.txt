Shockwaves hit the Serie A scene as AC Milan's preseason has been nothing short of dramatic.
The Rossoneri started with a bang, defeating their local rivals Inter Milan 2-0 in the Trofeo Milanello.
However, they suffered a 2-1 loss to another heavyweight in the Trofeo Luigi Berlusconi.
Their form looked shaky in a 1-1 draw against Bologna in the following match.
But, a resounding 3-0 victory over Cremonese lifted their spirits before the new season.
Pioli's team now gears up to face top-tier opponents in their upcoming friendly matches.
Can AC Milan maintain their winning momentum to dominate Serie A all season long?
