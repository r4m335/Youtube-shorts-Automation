GTA 6 leak hits the internet, but Take‑Two just fired back.
On August 20, Take‑Two filed DMCA subpoenas targeting Microsoft and Discord.
They want the identities of accounts that shared the leaked GTA VI footage.
Both companies must hand over data by September 4.
The subpoena includes IP addresses, device IDs, and account info.
They also target a Discord server linked to the leak.
They’re looking for the leaker, known as Cyberleek, and anyone who helped spread it.
