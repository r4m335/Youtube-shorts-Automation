Belgium's forests just went bonkers—wildfire apocalypse!
Firefighters say it’s the biggest ecological disaster the country’s ever seen.
The blaze scorched thousands of hectares in one of the biggest nature reserves.
Protected woodland and heathland are gone, risking plants and wildlife that thrive there.
The fire doubled in size in just 24 hours—sudden and relentless.
EU Civil Protection kicked in Saturday, with three helicopters and two water bombers.
Fire crews are on the front lines, working around the clock to contain the inferno.
