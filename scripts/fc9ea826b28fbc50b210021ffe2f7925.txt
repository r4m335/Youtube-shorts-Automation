$2 Trillion Fund in Jeopardy: What Happened to Norway's Economic Safety Net?
Norway's wealth fund has grown to a staggering record-high over the years.
It's the largest sovereign wealth fund globally, built from oil revenues.
This massive fund is used to support Norway's economy and social welfare system.
However, due to a global economic downturn, Norway's oil revenues are declining sharply.
As a result, experts warn that $2 trillion could be lost within a decade.
A dwindling fund would not only affect Norway's economy but also the global financial landscape.
Can Norway adapt to this new economic reality before it's too late?
